Our methodology

How we
approach learning

We don't teach AI tools. We build the human foundation that makes AI tools actually work — for your team, in your workflows, on your problems.

01
Start with meaning, not tools

Before anyone opens a laptop, we use Pre-Texts pedagogy — art-informed methods developed at Harvard that help teams think together, surface assumptions, and build the psychological safety needed to learn something genuinely new. This is what makes the technical learning actually land.

02
Build with real problems

Every session is anchored in your team's actual workflows. Participants don't practise on hypotheticals — they map real friction points, build real tools, and leave with something they can use on Monday morning. Learning happens by doing, not watching.

03
Leave something behind

Every programme generates tangible assets: session recordings, documented workflows, and shared team knowledge. The goal is for learning to compound inside your organisation long after the programme ends — not disappear the moment it's over.

Our philosophy

AI adoption is a culture challenge first,
a skills challenge second

Most AI training fails because it treats adoption as a technical problem. Weave Lab treats it as a human one. We use art-informed methods (Pre-Texts pedagogy, developed at Harvard) to build the cognitive flexibility teams need to work confidently with ambiguous, generative tools — now and as the landscape keeps changing.

✦

Teaching for Understanding Every session is built around real performances of understanding, not passive content consumption

✦

Pre-Texts Pedagogy Art-informed methods that build psychological safety and shared inquiry before technical learning begins

✦

Andragogy (Knowles) Adults learn best from real problems, with autonomy, at their own pace. Every build is anchored in actual work

✦

Infrastructure over events We measure success by what your organisation can do after we leave, not during the session
